thou lump of foul deformity!
the tartness of your face sours ripe grapes.
thou smell of mountain goat.
more of your conversation would infect my brain.
thou art a fusty nut with no kernel.
your wit makes wise things foolish.
I was searching for a fool when I found you.
You are not worth another word.
Thou hast no more brain than I have in my elbows.
Thou mis-shapen Dicke!
